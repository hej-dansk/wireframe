Frequently Asked Questions

What are PD2 and PD3?

PD2 (Prøve i Dansk 2) and PD3 (Prøve i Dansk 3) are official Danish language exams at B1 and B2/C1 level, used for residency, citizenship, and employment purposes.

How is Hejdansk different from other language apps?

Hejdansk is built specifically around the PD2/PD3 exam format — every lesson, quiz, and mock test mirrors what you'll actually face on exam day, backed by an honest readiness score instead of streaks and badges.

Can I try it before subscribing?

Yes — the Gratis Prøve (free trial) gives you a short reading exercise so you can experience the platform before signing up.

What does the AI writing feedback do?

Submit an essay in Danish and it's scored across 5 dimensions — grammar, vocabulary, coherence, task achievement, and register — with a straight pass/fail verdict, just like a real examiner.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes, on the Advanced and Pro plans — full terms are shown at checkout.

How long does it take to prepare for PD3?

Most learners follow the 12-Week Pass Plan, though this varies with your starting level and how much time you can study each week.
